04 / How it works

How we build each guide

  1. Pick a guide

    We find the real questions Pembroke Welsh Corgi owners ask in forums, groups and comments.

  2. Check the facts

    Every figure and source links to the origin so you can verify it yourself.

  3. Build a tool

    Each guide includes a small working tool — a planner, a checker, a calculator or a pedigree tree.

  4. Publish and review

    Written with AI help, checked by a person before it goes live. No exceptions.

Evidence-based approach. Every claim in our guides links to its source — an AKC breed profile, a grooming guide, a published study or a real owner thread. When data is thin, we say so. Cymru am byth.
